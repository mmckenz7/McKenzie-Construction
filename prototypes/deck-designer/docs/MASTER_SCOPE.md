# McKenzie Deck Designer master scope

North star: a polished McKenzie-owned configurator where a customer or employee can create a trustworthy visual design, compare alternatives, and turn the same authoritative design into deterministic quantities and, after approval, a McKenzie estimate. Target roughly 80–90% of the valuable user-facing TimberTech/3D Cloud workflow without unsafe engineering claims or enterprise catalog breadth.

## Permanent quality gates

- One design drives every projection.
- Every derived quantity is reproducible from recorded facts.
- Every build enforces the prototype import/network/environment isolation boundary.
- Visualization, estimate, and engineer/permit quantities remain distinct.
- No unlicensed assets or hard-coded production catalog dump.
- No production integration without a written contract and explicit approval.
- Other McKenzie OS tracks never wait on Deck Designer work.
